🐦 Sky Bounce

Tap or press Space to bounce up. Time your taps carefully to glide through the pipes!

How to Play

• Small taps = gentle lift
• Don't hit pipes or edges
• Hard mode is faster & darker

💡 Tips

Focus on the gap, not the bird. Anticipate the next pipe early.

Difficulty

Easy: Slower pipes, wider gaps
Hard: Fast pipes, narrow gaps, dark theme

🧱 Block Rush

Select a block piece below the board, then tap the grid to place it. Clear full rows and columns to score!

Controls

1. Tap a piece to select
2. Tap the board to place
3. Clear lines for points

📊 Scoring

Every block placed earns points. Clear lines for bonus points with combo multipliers!

Points

+1 per block
+50 per cleared line
Combos multiply your score!
