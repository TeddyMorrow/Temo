🐦 Sky Bounce

Tap or press Space to bounce. Choose Easy or Hard on the game screen.

How to play

• Avoid the pipes
• Keep away from the top and bottom
• Hard mode moves faster

💡 Tip

Watch the gap and tap early. Small controlled bounces beat panic tapping.

Scores

2. Tap grid for ghost preview
3. Tap again to place
