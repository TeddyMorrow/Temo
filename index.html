🐦 Sky Bounce

Tap or press Space to bounce. Choose Easy or Hard on the game screen.

How to play

• Avoid the pipes
• Keep away from the top and bottom
• Hard mode moves faster

💡 Tip

Watch the gap and tap early. Small controlled bounces beat panic tapping.

Scores

Easy and Hard mode save separate best scores.

🧱 Block Rush

Select a piece, tap the board to preview a shadow, then tap the same spot again to confirm.

New control

1. Pick a piece
2. Tap grid for ghost preview
3. Tap again to place

📊 Scoring

Clear full rows and columns. Clearing several lines in a row builds a combo multiplier.

Points

+1 per block
+50 per line × combo
Best score saves automatically
